A New Era for Welding Inspection

The CWI is Evolving

CWI 2.0: 50 Years Strong, Built for the Next 50

For 50 years, the AWS Certified Welding Inspector (CWI) has been the industry gold standard for verifying welded work. For the first time since its inception, the content and structure of the CWI are evolving, with a streamlined certification structure, a common examination framework and a new Welding Inspector in Training (WIIT) credential.

Why CWI is Evolving

Over the past 50 years, welding technology has advanced, inspection tools and practices have changed, and the responsibilities, knowledge, skills and abilities of the welding inspector have continued to evolve.

For the first time since its inception, AWS is updating the content and structure of the CWI exam and certification credential to reflect how advances in technology and inspection practices are changing the role of the welding inspector.

An evolution of the industry’s gold standard, not a replacement.

What’s Changing

CWI 2.0 introduces a streamlined structure that reflects the demands of modern welding inspection, and a clearer certification pathway.

Aligned with ISO/IEC 17024:2026

The CWI 2.0 program meets the ISO/IEC 17024:2026 standard, reinforcing CWI’s global leadership position.

Two exams instead of three

The CWI certification examination moves from the current three-part exam structure to two exams.

Welding Fundamentals Examination first

Candidates will complete the Welding Fundamentals Examination (WFE) before taking the CWI certification examination.

One common examination framework

The certification examination will be based on the Multi-Industry Sector Book of Specifications, giving all candidates a common framework.

New credential

Welding Inspector in Training (WIIT)

WIIT replaces CAWI and gives candidates who have passed the certification examination, but have not yet met the experience requirement, a structured, supervised path into the credential.

What’s Staying the Same

The foundation that made CWI the gold standard is not changing.

Same Body of Knowledge

The existing CWI Body of Knowledge remains in place.

Same education & experience requirements

Education and experience requirements are retained.

Same exam integrity

The rigor and integrity of the CWI examination remain.

Current program runs through 2027

Training, exam and credential continue throughout all of 2027 alongside CWI 2.0, with equal validity.
