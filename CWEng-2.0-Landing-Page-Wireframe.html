Brief Notes

Brief guardrails
  • Avoid “expert”, “expertise”, “competent”, “ensure higher salary” and similar claims.
  • Key message: redesigned program aligned with AWS B5.16:2025 and in conformance with ISO/IEC 17024.
  • If competitors come up (IWE, EWE, CSWIP): IWE/EWE are diplomas (completed training), not certification programs.
Audiences
  • Primary: welding, metallurgical, mechanical (welded systems) and materials engineers; Welding Engineering Technologists.
  • Secondary: employers in oil & gas, construction, aerospace & defense, heavy manufacturing; quality/engineering managers; code bodies.
  • Global: China has the most CWEngs and exams are offered in Chinese. Plan a Chinese-language version (hreflang).
